"We continue to stand shoulder to shoulder with our Gulf partners and the shipping industry," said Cooper.
"This visit underscores our strong commitment to continued freedom of navigation through the Strait of Hormuz."
American forces have successfully cleared the Strait of Hormuz's international shipping lanes of sea mines laid by Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), according to the U.S. Central Command.

Consumer prices have surged this year, with headline inflation across the U.S. rising at a 3.7% annual rate in September as energy costs jumped.
