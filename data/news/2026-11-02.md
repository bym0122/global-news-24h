Both the growth for the economy on the investment side and the wealth effect on the consumption side are all AI, he said.

That demand will lead to lower prices and higher spreads, though Microsoft&#x27;s deal attracted huge investor interest and was oversubscribed.

"That old tool from the Great Financial Crisis," he said. "Short it. You can trade two ways in [public] credit markets and you can&#x27;t do that in bank loans and private credit. But once they start going in public markets, you can do that."
Rosenberg said a 12% down day in Google is "not even on the chart" historically. What the CDS market is telling investors with the large-cap hyperscaler names, according to Rosenberg, is not that the prospect of default is high, but that as all the companies try to access more debt financing, the capacity of the banks and financial intermediaries to find counter-parties for the risk is becoming more limited.
"Think about financial markets and these big investment booms. They don&#x27;t move in one direction. They ratchet up, reset." American and Israeli forces have dealt a severe blow to its nuclear infrastructure—a significant military achievement.
